Results Screen — Mobile Notes

  • Loop cards are the primary element — full-width, scrollable vertically.
  • Waveform: 64px tall on mobile. Pre-computed peaks from DB (no client re-decode).
  • Play button left of waveform. Tap to audition before downloading.
  • Section markers on waveform: thin vertical lines (from section_label data).
  • Tags: 5 per loop — stem / BPM / key / section / energy class.
  • Download WAV: single-loop download. Drag handle: drag-to-DAW affordance.
  • Filter chips: horizontal scroll. Filter by stem type OR section label.
  • Loop pack ZIP download: prominent at top, above loop list.
  • All 12+ loops visible by scrolling — no pagination.
