Design note
Error states appear inline on the Job Progress screen, below the failed stage. Each card replaces the stage's status indicator with the error detail.
Download errors
URL ERR
DOWNLOAD_INVALID_URL
That doesn’t look like a YouTube link
Failed
The URL you pasted isn’t a valid YouTube link. stem-loops only works with standard youtube.com and youtu.be URLs — playlists and shorts aren’t supported yet.
What to do next
Open the YouTube video in your browser, copy the full URL from the address bar, and paste it again.
BLK ERR
DOWNLOAD_BLOCKED
YouTube is temporarily blocking this
Failed
YouTube flagged our request for this video. This is usually a rate-limit that clears on its own — it’s not a problem with your link.
What to do next
Wait 2–5 minutes, then retry. If it keeps failing, try a different video to confirm the service is running, then come back to this one.
TME OUT
DOWNLOAD_TIMEOUT
Couldn’t reach that video in time
Failed
The download took longer than expected and was stopped. This can happen with very long videos or during high server load.
What to do next
Try again in a moment. For best results, use videos under 10 minutes. Very long tracks (1hr+) are not supported.
18+ AGE
DOWNLOAD_AGE_RESTRICTED
This video is age-restricted
Failed
YouTube requires sign-in to access this video, which our downloader can’t do. Age-restricted content cannot be processed at this time.
What to do next
Try to find an unrestricted upload of the same track. If you own the audio, you can upload it directly once that feature is available.
LCK PRV
DOWNLOAD_PRIVATE
This video is private or unavailable
Failed
The video at this URL is set to private, has been deleted, or is unavailable in our region. We can only process publicly accessible videos.
What to do next
Double-check the URL is correct and the video is publicly visible. If it was public before, the uploader may have taken it down.
Processing errors
SEP ERR
SEPARATION_FAILED
Stem separation failed
Failed
Our AI model crashed while trying to split this audio into stems. This is rare and usually caused by unusual audio encoding or corrupt source data.
What to do next
Retry once — transient crashes often resolve themselves. If the same video keeps failing, try a different track to confirm the issue is track-specific.
EXT ERR
EXTRACTION_FAILED
Couldn’t find clean loops in this audio
Failed
Loop extraction completed but couldn’t identify any usable sections. This usually means the audio is too short, too variable in tempo, or heavily live-recorded with no clear structure.
What to do next
Try a track with a clearer beat structure — produced electronic or hip-hop tracks tend to work best. Live or orchestral recordings may not yield loops.
UPL ERR
UPLOAD_FAILED
Separated your stems but couldn’t save them
Failed
Stem separation completed successfully, but we hit an error storing the output files. Your results are not lost — the stems exist in temporary storage while you retry.
What to do next
Tap “Retry upload” — this only retries the save step and won’t re-run the model. If it fails again after 3 attempts, contact support with your job ID.
Catch-all
INT 500
INTERNAL_ERROR
Something went wrong on our end
Failed
An unexpected error occurred and the job couldn’t complete. This is a server-side issue — nothing you did caused this.
What to do next
Try again — most transient errors resolve on a second attempt. If this happens repeatedly, it may indicate a wider outage. We’re automatically notified when this error fires.
