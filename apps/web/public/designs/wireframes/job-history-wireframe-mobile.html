Job History — Mobile Notes

  • History is stored in localStorage (client-side) + signed cookie (server-side cross-device).
  • No login, no account — fully anonymous. History is per-browser.
  • Each entry shows: title (fetched at job creation), stems, bar length, loop count, status, date.
  • Status indicators: dot colour + text badge. Done (grey), Processing (blue), Failed (red).
  • Status stripe at top of card: thin 3px color band. Visual quick-scan.
  • Expiry warning: shown when < 24h remaining. Red date text + advisory note.
  • "Clear all" removes localStorage + logs out the signed cookie session.
  • Failed jobs show user-friendly error reason (from error_message_user), not error code.
  • Jobs still in progress show "Watch progress" → links to the progress page.
  • Share button: copies job URL to clipboard (same shareable link from progress screen).
  • Mobile-first: all interactions are touch-friendly 44px+ targets.
